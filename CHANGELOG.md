## 1.2.0 (MAJOR) — January 20, 2026

### New Pricing Tiers (Feature)

Introduced FREE tier and optimized Creator Pro plan for better value.

### Improved Face Swap (Feature)

More accurate face swapping with better blending and multi-face support. Expression morphing coming soon.

### A/B Testing Dashboard (Improvement, Preview)

Dashboard UI for comparing thumbnail variants. Automatic tracking integration coming soon.

## 1.1.5 (MINOR) — January 10, 2026

### Faster Generation Speed (Improvement)

2x faster thumbnail generation for Pro and Ultra Pro users with priority queue system.

### Fixed Text Rendering (Fix)

Resolved issue where custom text would occasionally appear blurry at high resolutions.

## 1.1.0 (MAJOR) — December 28, 2025

### Trending Insights (Feature, Preview)

Browse trending YouTube thumbnails by category and region. Full live data integration coming soon.

### Mobile App Beta (Improvement)

Released beta version of ThumPiks mobile app for iOS and Android.

## 1.0.5 (MINOR) — December 15, 2025

### Style Presets & Layouts (Improvement)

Added curated style presets and composition layouts across gaming, tech, lifestyle, and education niches.

### Export Quality (Fix)

Fixed 4K export quality issue affecting certain graphics card configurations.

### Face Swap Accuracy (Fix)

Improved face detection accuracy in low-light and side-angle scenarios.

## 1.0.0 (MAJOR) — November 20, 2025

### ThumPiks Launch (Feature)

Official public launch of ThumPiks with AI-powered thumbnail generation.

### Core Features (Feature)

Face swap, style library, text customization, and 1080p exports.

### User Dashboard (Feature)

Complete user dashboard with thumbnail history, analytics, and account management.
